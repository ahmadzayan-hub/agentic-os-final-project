FINDING F3 · blocker · P07
Category: denominator
Evidence: The 98.0% is "the mean of each vehicle's rate", which is an average of row rates. The standard (`brain/Concepts/Weighted KPIs.md`) calls this a defect. Vehicles with fewer scheduled hours get the same weight as vehicles with more. The register defines availability as available hours ÷ scheduled hours and expects 96.4%. The memo then says the register "understates" availability, which reverses the standard. The ratio of sums is the correct figure, and the row average is the artefact. The 98.0% also has no stated source or per-vehicle data.
Fix: Report 96.4% (available hours ÷ scheduled hours). Delete the claim that the register understates it, or show the per-vehicle hours and say why the two measures differ.

FINDING F7 · major · P13
Category: overclaim
Evidence: An AUC of 0.81 measures how well the model ranks failures. It does not show that the model "will prevent most failures". Prevention would need an intervention, a measured effect and a cost. The memo gives no baseline model, no held-out split, no precision or recall at a threshold, no base rate and no check for leakage. Leakage matters here because failure-prediction features often include post-failure maintenance records. The memo also does not say how many vehicles and failures the model was trained on.
Fix: Report the AUC against a baseline (for example the base rate or a simple rule) on a named held-out set, with precision and recall at the chosen threshold. Describe the model as moderate, with a leakage check. Delete "will prevent most failures", or label it a hypothesis.
