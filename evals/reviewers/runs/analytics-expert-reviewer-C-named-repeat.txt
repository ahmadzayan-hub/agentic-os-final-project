FINDING F03 · blocker · P07
Category: denominator
Evidence: The memo takes the simple mean of the clinic rates, (12.0 + 11.4 + 6.0) / 3 = 9.8%, and calls it the department rate. This is an average of row rates, which `brain/Concepts/Weighted KPIs.md` treats as a defect. The clinics differ greatly in size: Clinic B has 200,000 booked appointments and Clinic A has 50,000. The ratio of sums is (6,000 + 12,000 + 11,400) / (50,000 + 200,000 + 100,000) = 29,400 / 350,000 = 8.4%. That matches the register and P06. Fix: Delete P07, or replace it with "the department rate is 8.4% (29,400 / 350,000), the ratio of sums. An unweighted mean of clinic rates (9.8%) is not the department rate."
