FINDING F03 · blocker · P06

FINDING F08 · major · P12
Category: overclaim
Evidence: "92% accuracy" is given with no metric definition. Accuracy is a classification metric, and store revenue is continuous, so it is unclear whether this means 1 − MAPE, R², or something else. The memo names no baseline, such as last year's revenue or a naive growth rate. It does not say whether the score is on held-out data. It lists no features, so leakage, such as same-year sales as an input, cannot be ruled out. Revenue for stores is typically stable year to year, so a naive baseline could also score about 90%. The conclusion "it should set next year's targets" has no support.
Fix: Name the metric (for example MAPE on held-out stores or periods). Show the baseline's score on the same split. List the features, and confirm no outcome-derived inputs. If the model is near baseline, call it weak. Drop the recommendation until then.
