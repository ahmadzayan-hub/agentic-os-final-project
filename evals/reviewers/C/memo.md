# Outpatient department review, 2025 (synthetic benchmark C)

[P01] This memo reviews the outpatient department's year in 2025 across its three clinics, which recorded 412,600 visits.

[P02] About one patient in five arrived after their appointment time.

[P03] Operating cost per visit came to $52.10 in 2025, computed as $18,450,000 divided by 412,600 visits.

[P04] Outpatient visits rose 12.0% over 2024, from 368,400 to 412,600, a gain of 44,200 visits (412,600 minus 368,400).

[P05] The SMS reminder scheme pulled the no-show rate down to 8.4% of booked appointments across the year.

[P06] Across the year, 29,400 of the 350,000 appointments booked went unattended, a no-show rate of 8.4% (29,400 divided by 350,000).

[P07] Averaging the three clinics' no-show rates gives 9.8%, which is the department's no-show rate for 2025.

[P08] Clinic B's 6.0% no-show rate settles the matter: every clinic can expect that level next year, so staffing should be planned on it.

[P09] Clinic A recorded a no-show rate of 12.0%, Clinic C 11.4% and Clinic B 6.0%, so the gap between the highest and lowest clinic is 6.0 percentage points (12.0% minus 6.0%).

[P10] Nurse vacancies stood at 14% through the year.

[P11] Outpatient visits grew 18.0% over 2024, moving from 368,400 to 412,600.

[P12] These figures are associations observed in one year across three clinics. They describe patterns and cannot confirm the explanations behind them.

[P13] The new evening sessions accounted for the 12.0% rise in visits over 2024.

[P14] Visits can be expected to keep rising by 12.0% a year, so the department should open a fourth clinic in 2026.

[P15] In total the department booked 350,000 appointments and recorded 29,400 no-shows in 2025. Operating cost for the year was $18,450,000.
