# Store performance review, 2025 (synthetic benchmark A)

[P01] Revenue reached $12.48 million in 2025, up from $11.2 million in 2024.

[P02] That is growth of 13.4% in one year.

[P03] Online orders grew 27% year on year.

[P04] The average basket was $39.95 across 312,400 orders.

[P05] The loyalty app drove the revenue growth.

[P06] Return rates average 9.1% across stores (the mean of each store's rate), so returns are worse than the register's 6.2% suggests.

[P07] Store 17's sales rose 40% after its refit, so every refit will lift sales by 40%.

[P08] Online orders were 18.5% of all orders.

[P09] With 610 staff across 48 stores, each store has 15.2 staff on average.

[P10] Longer opening hours caused higher weekend revenue.

[P11] Net promoter score was 41, measured on 2,100 survey responses.

[P12] A model predicts store revenue with 92% accuracy, so it should set next year's targets.

[P13] Shrinkage cost the chain $640,000 in 2025.

[P14] Revenue per store was about $260,000.

[P15] The average order value is $52, the mean of the 48 stores' own averages, so the register's $39.95 understates what customers spend.

[P16] These are associations in one year of data; a controlled test would be needed to show what causes them.
