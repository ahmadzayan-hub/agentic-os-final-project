# Light-rail fleet reliability, 2025 (synthetic benchmark B)

[P01] The fleet of 40 vehicles ran 4.96 million km and recorded 1,240 failures.

[P02] That is one failure every 4,800 km.

[P03] Maintenance cost was $18.6 million, or $465,000 per vehicle.

[P04] Hot weather causes door faults to double in summer.

[P05] Door faults made up 31% of all failures.

[P06] Depot B's punctuality was 0.4 points better in March, which proves its new shift pattern works.

[P07] Availability averaged 98.0% across the 40 vehicles (the mean of each vehicle's rate), so the register's 96.4% understates it.

[P08] Punctuality was 98.1% for the year.

[P09] Delay minutes per failure were 52,300 ÷ 1,240 = 48.2 minutes.

[P10] Failures and delay minutes rose together in the summer months; the data does not show which drives which.

[P11] The new brake pads reduced failures by 12%.

[P12] Spare-parts stockouts cost $1.9 million.

[P13] A failure-prediction model reached an AUC of 0.81, so it will prevent most failures.

[P14] Mean distance between failures was 4,000 km.
